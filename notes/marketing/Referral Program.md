# Referral Program

Existing clients get $50 off their next job for each referred client who books. The referred client gets 10% off their first job — the maximum discount we allow under the [[Pricing Guide]].

Top referrers: the [[Hendricks Family]] (2 referrals) and Jenna at Seaside Cafe (3 leads from one post).

Referrals convert at 78%, far better than any paid channel. We track the source in the Job Intake Process and review results in the Monthly Cash Flow meeting.
