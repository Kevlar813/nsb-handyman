# Review Responses

Templates for replying to reviews on the [[Google Business Profile]].

5-star: Thank them by name, mention the specific job, and invite them back. Example: "Thanks, Linda! The deck came out great — enjoy it this summer."

3-star or below: Respond within 24 hours, apologize without arguing, offer a call, and schedule a free warranty visit if it's workmanship (see [[Warranty Policy]]).

Never mention prices or other clients by name in public replies.
