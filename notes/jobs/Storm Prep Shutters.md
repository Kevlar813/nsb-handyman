# Storm Prep Shutters

Seasonal job type. Each fall we install or inspect storm shutters for about 25 homeowners, mostly in the beachside zone and Oakview.

Pricing: $45 per opening to install, $25 per opening to inspect only. Average job is 12 openings.

Booked on Friday afternoons reserved for storm work per the [[Scheduling Rules]]. When a named storm is forecast, we text the shutter list and work existing clients first.

This job type is about 9% of annual revenue and a big part of September in the [[Monthly Cash Flow]].
