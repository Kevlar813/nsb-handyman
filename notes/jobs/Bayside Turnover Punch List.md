# Bayside Turnover Punch List

Standard checklist for [[Bayside Property Management]] unit turnovers:
- Patch nail holes and touch-up paint (unit paint codes on file)
- Replace burned-out bulbs, check smoke detectors
- Tighten/replace door hardware and cabinet hinges
- Re-caulk tub and kitchen sink
- Test all GFCIs, faucets, and the garbage disposal
- Photos of every room before and after

Average turnover is 4.5 hours. Goal is to keep it under 5 so the 72-hour turnaround holds. Scheduled Mondays per the [[Scheduling Rules]].
