# Martinez Drywall Patch

Client: [[Martinez Rental Portfolio]]. Completed September 2026.

Tenant moved out leaving a 3x4 ft hole behind a door plus a dozen anchor holes. Cut and patched, three coats of mud, sanded, primed, and painted.

Labor 7 hours across two visits (mud dry time). Materials from Coastal Lumber. Invoiced $742.

Dust got into the HVAC return, so the [[Safety Checklist]] now requires covering returns and wearing masks for all sanding.
