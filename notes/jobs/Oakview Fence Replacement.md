# Oakview Fence Replacement

Client: [[Oakview HOA]]. Completed June 2026.

Scope: removed 180 linear feet of failing wood pool fence and installed aluminum fence to meet pool code, plus two self-closing gates.

Fence panels ordered through [[Coastal Lumber]] with a 3-week lead time. Labor: 46 hours, two-person crew.

Invoiced $9,850, paid net-30 on time. One warranty callback for a sagging gate hinge (see [[Warranty Policy]]). Board was happy and asked us to quote the clubhouse deck next.
