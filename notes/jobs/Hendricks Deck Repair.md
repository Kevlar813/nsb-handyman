# Hendricks Deck Repair

Client: [[Hendricks Family]]. Completed April 2026.

Scope: replaced 14 rotted deck boards, 2 joists, and the stair stringers; sanded and stained with oil-based semi-transparent stain.

Materials from [[Coastal Lumber]]: pressure-treated 2x6 and 5/4 decking, $1,140 at cost. Labor: 22 hours across two days with Dan Rivera.

Total invoiced: $3,181 (labor $1,870, materials $1,311 with markup). 30% deposit collected up front because it was over $2,500. Lesson: stain needed 48 hours dry time, which pushed the job into a rain day — the Scheduling Rules now block two dry days for exterior stain jobs.
