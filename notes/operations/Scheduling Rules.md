# Scheduling Rules

- Arrival windows are 2 hours, first job 8:00-10:00.
- No more than 3 residential jobs per tech per day; drive time eats the margin otherwise.
- Bayside Property Management turnovers get priority on Mondays because their tenants move out on weekends (see [[Bayside Turnover Punch List]]).
- Friday afternoons are held for storm and emergency calls June-November.
- Any job needing two people (decks, fences, heavy doors) is booked only on days Dan Rivera is on the schedule.
- Rain days: exterior work is rescheduled by 7:00 AM with a text to the client.

Follows the [[Safety Checklist]] for anything involving ladders over 12 feet.
