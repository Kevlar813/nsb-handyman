# Pricing Guide

Our standard labor rate is $85/hour with a 2-hour minimum for residential calls. Commercial accounts like Bayside Property Management get a contracted rate of $78/hour in exchange for a guaranteed 20 hours a month.

Flat-rate items: TV mount $149, ceiling fan swap $175, door rehang $140, faucet replacement $160 (customer-supplied fixture). Materials are billed at cost plus 15%, pulled from [[Coastal Lumber]] where possible.

Trip charge of $35 applies outside the core zones defined in the Service Area note. Any job estimated over $2,500 needs a written quote and a 30% deposit per the [[Invoicing Workflow]]. Discounts are capped at 10% and only through the Referral Program or the Spring Promo.
