# Service Area

Core zones (no trip charge): the beachside neighborhoods, downtown, and the Oakview subdivision. Extended zones (+$35 trip charge, see [[Pricing Guide]]): the inland ranch roads and anything more than 25 minutes from the shop.

We do not take jobs across the county line unless it's an existing client such as the Martinez Rental Portfolio. Dan Rivera covers the north routes on Tuesdays and Thursdays; Kevin takes the south side.

Storm season (June-November) we reserve Friday afternoons for emergency calls, per the [[Scheduling Rules]].
