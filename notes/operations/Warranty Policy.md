# Warranty Policy

Workmanship is guaranteed for 12 months. Materials carry the manufacturer's warranty only.

Not covered: damage from storms, tenant abuse, or other contractors working on the same item. Customer-supplied parts are labor-only warranty for 90 days.

Warranty callbacks are scheduled within 5 business days and are never billed. We track callback rate monthly; anything above 3% of jobs triggers a review with Dan Rivera. The Oakview Fence Replacement had one callback for a sagging gate, fixed in 30 minutes.
