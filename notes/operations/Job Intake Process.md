# Job Intake Process

1. Lead comes in by phone, website form, or Google Business Profile message.
2. Within 1 business hour, call back and fill the intake sheet: address, scope, photos, access notes, pets.
3. Check the address against the Service Area note and quote the trip charge if needed.
4. If the job is under 4 hours, book directly using the [[Scheduling Rules]]. Over 4 hours, schedule a free 20-minute estimate visit.
5. Send confirmation text with arrival window and the [[Warranty Policy]] link.
6. Log the lead source so the Referral Program and Spring Promo can be tracked in Monthly Cash Flow reviews.

Target: 60% of estimates convert to booked jobs. Last quarter we hit 54%.
