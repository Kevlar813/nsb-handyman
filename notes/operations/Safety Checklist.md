# Safety Checklist

Every job, before tools come out:
- Walk the site, note trip hazards, pets, and overhead lines.
- Ladder over 12 ft: second person on site, ladder tied off.
- Shut off breaker for any electrical work and test with the non-contact tester.
- Dust masks for drywall sanding (the Martinez Drywall Patch job taught us this the hard way).
- Respirator and ventilation for any oil-based stain, like the Hendricks Deck Repair.
- First aid kit and fire extinguisher checked monthly in the truck — tied to the [[Insurance Policy]] requirements.

Incidents get written up the same day and reviewed in the Monday meeting.
