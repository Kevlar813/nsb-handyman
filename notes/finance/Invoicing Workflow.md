# Invoicing Workflow

- Residential: invoice sent from the truck on completion; card, check, or ACH. Due on receipt.
- Jobs over $2,500: 30% deposit at booking, balance on completion (per the [[Pricing Guide]]).
- Commercial/HOA: monthly statement on the 1st; Bayside is net-15, Oakview HOA net-30.
- Reminders at 7 and 21 days past due; at 45 days, phone call from Kevin; at 60 days, work is paused.

Materials receipts from Coastal Lumber are attached to each invoice so markup is transparent. Reviewed weekly against the [[Monthly Cash Flow]].
