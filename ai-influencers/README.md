Ten fictional AI personas, one for each of the biggest creator niches on Facebook and Instagram.

| 9 | Home Improvement & DIY | Nate Sullivan, 42 | @fixitnate.ai |
| 10 | Comedy & Entertainment | Rosie Delgado, 26 | @rosie.reacts.ai |

Each persona includes: bio (fits Instagram's 150-char limit), backstory, personality and voice,
visual identity (palette, signature look, image-generation prompt and negative prompt), target
audience, content pillars, format mix, Instagram/Facebook posting cadence, three sample posts,
monetization, brand fits and guardrails.

Nate Sullivan (Home & DIY) is built to feed leads to NSB Handyman: his tutorials end with a
"when to call a pro" line.

## Files

- `personas.js`: the persona data (works in the browser and in Node)
- `index.html`: gallery of the personas; open it directly in a browser
- `validate.js`: checks the data. Run `node ai-influencers/validate.js`

## Disclosure rules

- "AI" appears in every bio. Every post uses Meta's AI info label and `#AIgenerated`.
- Sponsored posts use Meta's Paid Partnership tool and `#ad` (FTC endorsement guides).
- No persona looks like a real person, and no persona shows photorealistic children.
- Before you create the accounts, check that each handle is free. Availability has not been checked.
